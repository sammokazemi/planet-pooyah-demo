Questions parents ask

I don't speak any Persian. Can I still start?

Yes. The first lesson starts at the first letter, Alef. Every word is shown with its sound, its spelling in English letters and its meaning.

We already speak Persian at home. Will it be too easy?

Speaking and reading are different skills, and most of the path is about reading and writing. A placement check at the start skips the letters you already know.

Is it Persian or Farsi?

Both. Fârsi is the language's name in Persian; in English it's usually called Persian. It's the language spoken in Iran.

Is it only for children?

No. It's simple and playful enough for a 5-year-old, and adults learn with it too: grown-ups who never learned to read Persian, and people who married into an Iranian family and want the culture as well as the language.

What does it run on?

Any modern web browser, on a phone, a tablet or a computer. Progress is saved to your account, so you can pick up on another device.

What do you do with my child's data?

We collect as little as we can: your email, plus your child's made-up name, avatar, age range and lesson progress. There is no chat and no public profile. Read the details in our privacy policy.
